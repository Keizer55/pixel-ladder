# The Pixel Ladder Philosophy

## Why a Pig?

The pig mascot is a nod to the "Porco Rosso" aesthetic—a blend of technical craftsmanship and a slightly whimsical, adventurous spirit. In our world, the pig is a master painter, meticulously climbing his ladder to fix the "broken" pixels of the digital world.

## Why In-Browser AI?

Most AI tools today are "Cloud-First." They take your data, process it on a distant server, and send it back. 

Pixel Ladder is **"Local-First."** 
- **Privacy**: Your family photos or private designs never leave your computer.
- **Ownership**: You are using your own hardware to perform the work.
- **Transparency**: There are no hidden costs or "credits" to buy. You own the compute.

## The Aesthetic Choice

We chose a pixel-art theme because it celebrates the building blocks of digital imagery. By showing the "big pixels" in the UI, we contrast them with the "smooth results" of the AI, making the transformation feel more tangible.
