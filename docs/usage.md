# User Guide

Welcome to Pixel Ladder! Here is how to get the most out of the tool.

## 🖼️ How to Upscale an Image

1.  Go to the **Quick Scale** tab.
2.  Drag and drop your image or click the upload box.
3.  Select a model: **X2** doubles the resolution, **X4** quadruples it, and **X4 Anime** / **Pixel art** are ×4 variants for illustration and pixel art. Each card shows the output size.
4.  Click **Upscale now**. You can **Cancel** while the "Neural net" is working.
5.  Click **Download** to save your high-res image, or **Use in Print Studio →** to carry it straight to the next step.

## 🖨️ Preparing for Print

If you want to print a photo but aren't sure if it's high enough resolution:

1.  Go to the **Print Studio** tab.
2.  Enter your target physical size (e.g., 20cm x 30cm).
3.  Select your **DPI** (300 is recommended for most prints).
4.  The app will show you the **Required Pixels**.
5.  Upload your image to the **Crop Studio** on the right (an image loaded or upscaled in another tab is already there).
6.  Adjust the crop box. It is locked to the correct aspect ratio.
7.  Check the **Crop Preview** to see if the composition looks good.
8.  Now you know exactly what size your image needs to be! If the crop is too small, the warning says by how much and **Upscale in Quick Scale →** takes you there with a suitable model selected.

## 🧱 Planning a Wall

In **Wall Setup**, add frames and give each one an image with **Load image** or **Use current image**. If an image is too small for its frame, **Upscale in Quick Scale →** upscales it and **Replace in frame N →** puts the result back.

## 🌐 Language

Use the **EN | ES** selector in the top right corner to switch between English and Spanish. The first visit follows your browser language and your choice is remembered.

## 🌓 Changing the Vibe

Use the theme button next to it to switch between **Dark Mode** (Neon Green) and **Light Mode** (Warm Earth). Dark mode is great for focus, while Light mode is easier on the eyes in bright environments.
